# A. Question and design

The question was whether better risk information could buy better protection for a financial-sector equity long/short portfolio after costs. The intended chain ran from observable prices and funding characteristics to risk forecasts, then from forecasts to protection decisions. Forecast accuracy and economic hedge value were separate hypotheses. Stock-selection alpha was outside the study. These intentions come from the [design rationale reconstruction](review/design_rationale_reconstruction.md).

That document is a post-hoc reconstruction written after the results were known. It is not the original design document or evidence of advance commitment. Its statement that it excludes subsequent findings describes its reconstruction method. It does not restore contemporaneous provenance. I use it to explain the intended question and accept responsibility for gaps between that question and the implemented test.

The framework joined controlled option hedging, historical model-contract replay, chronological variance forecasts, filing-aware features and equity overlays. It tracked stock, options and financed cash. Historical comparisons held the executed base book fixed across protective overlays and included reduced equity as a benchmark. Training estimated relationships; validation selected candidates; locked policies then faced test observations. The historical options were hypothetical European contracts priced from trailing volatility. These are the implemented boundaries in the [publication](final_findings/research_release_2_publication.md).

The expectation was that funding differences could distinguish vulnerability among firms facing similar sector conditions. Better information would then justify selective insurance spending while preserving participation in gains. Neither expectation guaranteed success. The reconstruction separates forecasting, economic and research success. Reconciled accounting and an honestly bounded negative answer satisfy the research standard without satisfying the economic standard.

The design therefore needed a demanding comparator: what insurance adds beyond holding less equity. It also needed an objective that charged protection for surrendered upside. The examiner exposes where implementation weakened those intentions: pooled funding levels, infrequent adaptive decisions and omitted reporting of the frozen half-size benchmark. [Examiner](review/examiner_pass1_and_2.md).

# B. What the frozen release found

The release remains conditional; full empirical certification remains open. The [publication](final_findings/research_release_2_publication.md) states its conclusion:

> A controlled hedging experiment identifies a favorable cost–risk tradeoff. Historical extensions show that forecast improvements depend on metric and horizon, while model-priced option protection depends materially on pricing, exposure and evaluation-window assumptions. Incremental economic hedge value remains unestablished.

The controlled policy passed an expected financed stock-cost screen, not a pathwise spending cap. Its held-out comparison is stated precisely:

> This supports a conditional cost–risk tradeoff. It does not establish equivalence with daily hedging, historical alpha or robustness to an unspecified physical return process.

Forecast conclusions remain:

> Price-only ridge improves QLIKE relative to EWMA at both horizons, with favorable pointwise intervals. At five sessions its MSE is 29.3% worse as a point estimate, but the paired MSE interval includes zero. At twenty sessions, pointwise intervals favor ridge for both QLIKE and MSE. These metrics weight errors differently; neither result alone establishes improved hedging.

> The liability ratio provides a small horizon-specific gain; the tested deposit proxy worsens incremental QLIKE at both horizons. This concerns the proxy, model and cohort, not the general relevance of funding vulnerability.

Historical overlay conclusions remain:

> Reduced equity is a strong benchmark. Purchased-put tail differences versus unhedged are inconclusive under the reported pointwise intervals. The collar reduces modeled tail loss, but substantially changes exposure and sacrifices upside: mean initial local dollar delta changes from +50.401% to −5.385% of capital. Delta is not market beta, and no attribution establishes that the entire protection benefit is explained by reduced exposure.

> Purchased short-sleeve calls can reduce short-sleeve losses while worsening whole-book downside ES.

The historical tail averages the worst six of 23 separate accounts. It differs from simulated ES95. Premium allowances size decisions; they do not enforce execution caps. These definitions and the quotations above come from the [publication](final_findings/research_release_2_publication.md); the [claims supplement](final_findings/research_release_2_claims_and_errata.md) controls interpretation of older reports.

Post-freeze [display errata](review/errata_post_freeze.md) record direct rounding of selected ES95 as 3,327.94 rather than 3,327.95; validation offset 21 as 4.095 rather than 4.096; and offset 42 as −0.231 rather than −0.230. Frozen documents remain unchanged. No conclusion changes.

Accounting reconstruction supports terminal outcomes, not executable prices or every intrawindow mark. Post-hoc comparisons with reduced equity do not establish overall dominance because purchased puts retain more upside. Overlapping shifted windows add no independent crises. The publication states the breadth boundary:

> More names do not guarantee lower risk when composition and factor exposures change.

# D. The sharpened reading

This is post-hoc examination, not a replacement result. The frozen conclusions in B remain the publication. The examiner changes the interpretation by testing explanations the original reporting left unresolved. I accept the demand for stronger comparators and reject declarations of universal failure. The [claims supplement](final_findings/research_release_2_claims_and_errata.md) is right that inconclusive evidence does not establish useless protection.

The omitted half-size book matters. It was computed and independently reconstructed, then excluded by a reporting flag. Its comparison weakens the collar's claim to practical distinction. The examiner reports collar tail loss of 2.906% against 2.637% of capital per window, but the paired interval crosses zero. That supports an alternative, not equivalence or dominance. A rally loss counts as a bad state for this mandate: protection must answer for its own losses as well as baseline distress. The [examiner](review/examiner_pass1_and_2.md) supports this reading, not a completed causal attribution to exposure.

The reconstruction supplies no cost-matching justification for the budget, strike gap and equity cut, and no required survival loss. I cannot invent either after observing outcomes. The [reconstruction](review/design_rationale_reconstruction.md) expected funding characteristics to distinguish firms. Issuer intercepts and a pooled level coefficient instead tested within-bank movements under a shared sign. That mismatch is a design failure. The [diagnostics](review/examiner_diagnostics.md) support calibration drift and show deviation intervals spanning zero. Their declaration of “no” variance information goes beyond those intervals. I believe the frozen adverse score and the narrower diagnostic explanation together; neither cancels the other.

The historical positive put evidence now turns on affordable coverage in deep losses. The examiner's price explanation remains unverified without quotes. It does not invalidate the controlled hedge result. Low estimated detection power weakens any argument from test non-significance, but the power calculation itself fits exposed, overlapping outcomes. It is a design warning rather than independent confirmation. These boundaries follow the [examiner](review/examiner_pass1_and_2.md) and [diagnostics](review/examiner_diagnostics.md).

Interim protection deserves measurement because expiry and distress are different objectives. Existing marks establish neither liquidation value nor funding capacity. Likewise, rate sensitivity cannot identify an upper bound when pricing and cash rates move together. I prefer the release's conditional language to those stronger examiner claims.

The adaptive signal's infrequent sampling also limits the timing verdict. The examiner's later account qualifies its earlier claim that a necessary condition failed. Better temporal alignment deserves a future protocol, not a rescue of exposed performance. The research achievement remains disciplined accounting; the unresolved economic question now has a narrower path through measurement. [Examiner](review/examiner_pass1_and_2.md).

# E. Limitations

- **Model prices, no observed implied volatility.** Historical options are hypothetical European Black–Scholes contracts valued with trailing-return volatility. No historical option quotes or implied-volatility surface were acquired. Accounting agreement does not validate insurance affordability, spreads or executable fills. [Publication](final_findings/research_release_2_publication.md).
- **Loss depth and the strike gap.** The statement that “the test period's deepest loss never reached the strike gap” conflicts with the sources and cannot be included as fact. The examiner reports a deepest test long-sleeve return of −16.2%, a 10% strike gap, and four test windows beyond that gap. Aggregate sleeve loss is not an individual option's moneyness. The supported limitation is sparse deep-loss observations and inadequate detection power under the examiner's descriptive model, not failure ever to cross the gap. [Examiner](review/examiner_pass1_and_2.md), [diagnostics](review/examiner_diagnostics.md).
- **Liquidity is unmodelled.** Short proceeds count as cash. Historical overlays therefore record zero borrowing, which does not establish available collateral, borrow capacity or usable distress liquidity. The controlled short-call account is a different account and does report borrowing. [Phase report](PHASE1_REPORT.md).
- **Historical reproduction has explicit exclusions.** Canonical current-code outputs were reproduced from exact saved inputs. Superseded overlay and feature runs lack their original source versions; an earlier incomplete feature snapshot lacks a complete matching source-and-completion record. Those records are hash-only. Acquisition utilities cannot regenerate historical vendor and filing vintages through live queries. Do not describe every historical run as reproducible. [Experiment inventory](EXPERIMENTS.md), [phase report](PHASE1_REPORT.md).
- **Missing CRR pricer.** The original CRR callable is absent. The legacy method comparison requires it explicitly; no replacement pricing implementation was supplied. This limitation concerns a reference comparison, not the canonical publication experiments. [Experiment inventory](EXPERIMENTS.md), [phase report](PHASE1_REPORT.md).
- **Pre-commitment provenance is limited.** The shifted-window `pre_run_commitment.json` was written by the run itself, 61 seconds before completion. That supports script ordering, not independently recorded advance registration. I accept the examiner's qualification of the release's “Before running” account; this is separate from the controlled policy's independently reconstructed selection lock. [Examiner](review/examiner_pass1_and_2.md), [claims supplement](final_findings/research_release_2_claims_and_errata.md).
- **Redistribution rights remain unverified.** Vendor snapshots are excluded from current-tree tracking but remain in local files and historical Git objects. The software license does not grant rights to third-party data. [Phase report](PHASE1_REPORT.md).
- **Sparse, dependent and selected observations.** Separate windows are not a chained investment record. Shifted windows overlap. Current-survivor coverage omits failed and acquired institutions; pointwise intervals do not address multiplicity or selection uncertainty. Macro information and forecast-to-hedge economic value remain untested. [Publication](final_findings/research_release_2_publication.md), [claims supplement](final_findings/research_release_2_claims_and_errata.md), [examiner](review/examiner_pass1_and_2.md).
- **Financing and interim valuation remain incomplete.** Dividends, short-dividend payments, realistic borrow, margin, option spreads and American exercise are omitted. Daily NAV paths were not saved for the examiner; existing drawdown summaries use uncertified marks and initial-capital normalization. The saved rate sensitivity moves cash and pricing together. [Publication](final_findings/research_release_2_publication.md), [examiner](review/examiner_pass1_and_2.md).
- **Evidence coverage is bounded.** The examiner initially could not locate some episode and forecast evidence in the review folder; its later pass accepts the lineage answer. That resolves location, not every certification gap: the financing-adjusted sleeve capture remains an owner check, forecast reconstruction output was not archived, and bundle verification lacks the listed `.DS_Store`. The reconstruction itself is post-hoc testimony. [Examiner](review/examiner_pass1_and_2.md), [phase report](PHASE1_REPORT.md), [design reconstruction](review/design_rationale_reconstruction.md).

# G. Abstract

This project asks whether better risk forecasts produce better portfolio protection after costs. It combines controlled hedging simulations, chronological financial-sector forecasts and historical option overlays with cash accounting. The frozen conclusion is: “Incremental economic hedge value remains unestablished.” [Publication](final_findings/research_release_2_publication.md).

Controlled hedging identified a favorable cost–risk tradeoff. Historical forecast gains depended on metric and horizon. Model-priced protection depended on prices, exposure and evaluation windows. Independent examination exposed an omitted smaller-book comparator, funding-feature calibration drift and weak power to detect protection benefits. Those post-hoc findings refine interpretation without replacing frozen results. [Examiner](review/examiner_pass1_and_2.md), [diagnostics](review/examiner_diagnostics.md).

The engineering achievement is reproducible accounting with explicit historical exclusions. The economic limitation is unverified option prices and unmodelled liquidity. The next step is a real-quote check before further strategy design. The work demonstrates research judgment: reconcile results, preserve adverse evidence, distinguish measurement from inference and spend the next research effort on the assumption that decides value. [Phase report](PHASE1_REPORT.md).
